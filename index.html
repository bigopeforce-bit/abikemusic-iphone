Offline pulse

How to get music offline
  1. Open the track in YouTube Music with the button below.
  2. In the YouTube Music app, tap Download (Premium may be required).
  3. Come back here any time — the app shell itself works offline once cached.

This PWA can cache its own screens for offline viewing, but it cannot download YouTube songs or play them from your device storage.
